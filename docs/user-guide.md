# User Guide

This guide covers everything you need to know about using Swells — capturing
what you notice, and making sense of it together.

---

## What Swells is for

Swells turns small, everyday observations into a shared picture of what your
organisation is sensing. You add short notes about things you notice; Swells
reads them, groups related ones into **signals**, and presents the result
through a set of complementary views. Over time you can see which themes are
rising, where the mood is warm or cool, and what's worth paying attention to.

There's no right or wrong observation. The value comes from many people noticing
many small things.

---

## Spaces

A **space** is your team's shared home. All observations, signals, and
reflections live inside a space.

- **Switch spaces** using the space name in the top-left of the header.
- **Create a new space** from the same menu ("Create new space").
- **Space settings** (the gear icon, available to admins) let you rename the
  space, edit its description, and choose its visual environment.

### Members and roles

Admins can invite people and manage who can do what:

- **Owner / Admin** — full control, including settings and members.
- **Facilitator** — can add observations and help shape the space.
- **Observer** — can add observations.
- **Viewer** — can see the space but not contribute.

To invite someone, open space settings, enter their email and choose a role.
You'll get a link to share. Invitations expire after seven days.

## Capturing an observation

Press **I noticed something** (top-right on desktop, or the **+** button on
mobile) to open the capture window.

You can include any combination of:

- **Text** — describe what you observed. A gentle prompt suggests what to notice
  if you're not sure where to start.
- **A photo** — attach one or more images.
- **Voice** — record straight from your device, or upload an existing audio
  file.
- **A file** — attach a document (PDF, text, or CSV).

You don't need to write anything if you attach media — a photo or a voice note
on its own is a perfectly good observation.

### What happens after you submit

Once you send an observation, Swells quietly does some work in the background:

- **Voice notes are transcribed** so their words become part of the picture.
- **Images are read** — Swells describes them and pulls out any text they
  contain.
- **Documents are read** for their text.
- Your observation is then compared with others and, where it's related, joined
  into a **signal**.

This takes a few moments. Your observation appears straight away in the River;
the richer connections show up shortly after.

## The views

Swells shows the same underlying observations through several lenses. Switch
between them using the navigation in the header (or the bottom bar on mobile).

### River — the flow of observations

The River is the running stream of everything noticed by your team, newest
flowing in over time. Each card shows the observation, who added it, when, any
attached media, and the signal it belongs to.

You can **search** the River and filter by **signal strength**, **mood**, and
**source** (see [Filtering by source](#filtering-by-source)). Click a signal tag
on a card to see just the observations behind that signal.

### Signals — the landscape

The Signals view shows the patterns that have emerged. At the top, a **terrain**
graphic shows your most common themes rising and falling across the last 30
days — hover any point to see how active each theme was on that day.

Below the terrain, each **signal** is a card showing its name, what it means,
its strength (strong, emerging, or weak), its direction (strengthening, steady,
or new), and how many observations and people are behind it. Expand a card to
read the actual observations that make up the signal, and jump to any of them in
the River.

If your space is new, the terrain shows a friendly note until enough
observations have been added for themes to appear.

### Constellation — the map

The Constellation is a living map of your signals as points of light, with
related signals connected by gentle threads.

- **Drag** anywhere to pan the map around.
- **Click a signal** to open a panel on the right with its details and every
  observation behind it. The map gently shifts the signal into view so the panel
  never hides it.
- The observations orbit the signal as **numbered dots**, and each dot matches a
  numbered entry in the side panel — hover an entry to highlight its dot.
- Press **Escape** or click empty space to close the panel.

### Sentiment — the temperature

The Sentiment view shows the emotional temperature of your observations over
time. Warm means energy, excitement, urgency; cool means calm, reflective,
uncertain — neither is better.

- A **heatmap** colours each day by its overall mood. Switch between **daily**
  and **weekly** views.
- Click any day to read the observations behind it.
- A **distribution bar** summarises the month, and **insight cards** highlight
  what's running hot, where energy is building, and what's cool and uncertain.
- Use **Compare** to set this month against the previous one.

Until your space has observations with sentiment, this view shows a brief note
rather than sample data.

### Reflect — going deeper

As signals shift, Swells generates **reflection prompts** — open questions that
invite you to think more deeply about what's emerging. Prompts are tagged by
depth: what's happening, why it's happening, or what it says about how we see
the world.

Share your reflection on any active prompt. Your response stays visible with the
prompt, and — importantly — it feeds back in: a considered reflection is treated
as a new observation, so it can strengthen the signal that prompted it, or even
help surface a new one. Older prompts and their responses are kept under "Past
reflections."

### Timeline — everything in one stream

The Timeline is a single chronological feed of everything that's happened in the
space: observations, signal changes, and reflection prompts. Search it, or
filter by source.

## Filtering by source

Several views (River, Signals, Constellation, and Timeline) include a **Source**
filter so you can focus on where observations came from:

- **All sources** — everything.
- **Direct only** — just the observations added directly by your team.
- **Collections only** — just responses gathered through a collection link.
- **Reflections only** — just the responses people wrote to reflection prompts.
- **A specific collection** — responses from one particular collection.

The Source filter only appears once there's something to filter by.

## Collections — gathering observations from anyone

Collections let you gather observations from a wide group of people — a team, an
event, a community — **without anyone needing to sign up**. It works a little
like a quiet, reflective version of a live poll.

### Creating a collection

1. Open the **Collect** tab.
2. Press **New collection**.
3. Give it a question or prompt (for example, "What did you notice this week?"),
   and optionally a short description.
4. Optionally set a **close date** and a **response cap**.
5. Optionally turn on **moderation** if you want to review responses before they
   appear.
6. Press **Create** — you'll get a shareable link to copy.

### What respondents see

Anyone with the link opens a clean, simple page with your question. They can
respond with text, a photo, a voice recording or audio file, and an optional
name — then submit. They can add another response if they wish. No account, no
sign-in.

### Managing a collection

From the Collect tab you can:

- **Copy the link** to share it again.
- **Open or close** the collection to submissions.
- See each collection's **response count** at a glance.
- Open a collection to read its responses, adjust its settings, or delete it.

### Moderation

If moderation is on, new responses arrive as **pending** and don't appear
anywhere until you approve them. Open the collection to see the review queue and
**Approve** or **Reject** each one. Approved responses flow into your space and
its signals just like any other observation; rejected ones are hidden.

Responses gathered through a collection appear throughout Swells (River, Signals,
Constellation) and can be isolated at any time using the Source filter.

---

## Notifications

The bell icon in the header tells you when something changes — a new reflection
prompt, a signal shifting strength, or new activity. Open it to read recent
notifications and jump straight to the relevant view, and mark them all as read
in one tap.

---

## Plans and limits

Swells is offered in tiers (Individual, Team, and Organisation) that differ in
how many people can share a space and how many observations you can add each
month. New accounts start with a trial; a banner reminds you when it's running
low, and you can upgrade or manage billing from there.

Reflection responses and collection submissions count as observations against
your monthly allowance, since they become part of your space's sensing.

---

## Referrals

You can invite others to Swells from the **Referrals** page in the account menu.
