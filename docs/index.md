# Swells

Swells helps people sense what's emerging before it becomes obvious.

You and your team capture small **observations** — a feeling, a conversation, a
moment, a pattern. Swells listens for what those observations have in common,
gathers the related ones into **signals**, and shows you the shape of what's
stirring beneath the surface: where energy is building, what's running hot,
what's quietly shifting.

It's not a survey tool or a dashboard of metrics. It's a way to notice together.

## Getting started

1. Sign in (or create an account) and you'll land in your **space** — your
   team's shared home for observations.
2. Press **I noticed something** and describe what you saw. Don't worry about
   what it means.
3. Come back to the different views — **River**, **Signals**, **Constellation**,
   **Sentiment**, **Reflect**, **Timeline** — to watch patterns emerge over time.

New here? The [User Guide](user-guide.md) walks through everything you can do.
